Chart: Volcano

A volcano plot is useful for identifying variables that exhibit both large magnitude changes and high statistical significance.

X-axis (log2 fold change): Shows the magnitude and direction of change. Genes or features shifted to the right indicate upregulation (increased expression), while those shifted to the left indicate downregulation (decreased expression).

Y-axis (-log10 p-value): Represents the statistical significance of the change. Higher points indicate more significant changes.

NOTE: Drag and drop only one variable with numerical values for X-axis and another one for Y.
