Multivariate: Ward Hierarchical Clustering

Ward hierarchical clustering repeatedly merges the pair of clusters that produces the smallest increase in within-cluster variance. The dendrogram shows the merge distances; the selected cluster count colors the groups and summarizes them below.

NOTE: Select at least one numerical measurement variable. An optional row-label variable identifies observations in the dendrogram. Choose how many clusters to display.
