Chart: Box

A box chart, also known as a box-and-whisker plot, is a standardized way of displaying the distribution of numerical data based on a five-number summary: minimum, first quartile, median, third quartile, and maximum. It highlights the central tendency, variability, and potential outliers within the dataset.

NOTE: Drag and drop at least two variables with numerical values as a group to show the distributions.
